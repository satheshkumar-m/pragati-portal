The AI part, explained for parents

Your child will not be taught to let AI do their homework. They will learn that an AI model is a program that predicts the next word, that it can be fluent and wrong at the same time, and how to use it the way a good student uses a well-read friend: for explanations, drafts and practice, never for facts they have not checked.

Week 1 includes a recorded demo of an AI model running on an ordinary PC, one word at a time. No magic box.
